# Rubric — outreach DM/email (15 per issue)
1. Two sentences + link. No pitch.
2. References THEIR pool's section or the finding most relevant to them.
3. One specific observation proving a human read their situation.
4. Opens "Hey [Name] --", closes "Kaï". Zero banned words.
5. No Resolve-graph contacts (checked against list source column).
6. Sent BY Kaï, from Kaï's account, after edit. Never auto-sent.
7. Reply → Cal.com link with the "10 conversations to sharpen the next issue" frame.
8. Every reply logged verbatim to memory/episodic/ same day.
9. Detectably-generated tone = fail. In a 60-firm village one burned DM is expensive.
10. If we said it in a prior DM to the same person, we don't repeat it.
