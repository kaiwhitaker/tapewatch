# Tapewatch outreach templates — DRAFTS, confirm-tier
All of these are skeletons. Nothing goes out without: (a) the real finding filled in, (b) your edit, (c) your send. Two sentences + link is the ceiling for cold DMs. No pitch in message one.

## DM to a curator whose pool is in Monitor #1 (the core motion, playbook Step 5)

> Hey [Name] -- wrote up [pool] in the first Tokenized Credit Monitor; the section on [specific structural point, e.g. "the redemption sleeve vs. receivables duration"] is probably the part worth your two minutes: [link]. No ask -- flagging it since it's your pool.

## DM to a curator NOT in issue #1 (relevance play)

> Hey [Name] -- first Tokenized Credit Monitor is out; the [finding most relevant to their strategy, e.g. "NAV-oracle chain map across five pools"] likely overlaps with how you think about [their vault/mandate]: [link]. Curious if the framing matches what you see from the inside.

## Reply-to-engagement → call (playbook Step 6)

> Appreciate that. I'm doing 10 conversations with curators to sharpen issue #2 -- 20 minutes, no pitch, mostly me asking how you actually monitor what you're staked on. [Cal.com link] if useful.

## The teardown offer — only at the moment of pain, on a call or in reply to a stated gap (Step 7)
Say it, don't send it, where possible. If written:

> For the pool you mentioned -- I do a loan-level teardown: every position mapped against the five patterns, severity × confidence on each finding, methodology appendix. $2,500, five business days, delivered as a memo + 45-min readout. Want me to scope it?

## Analyst/amplifier DM (Tier 4 — distribution, not sales)

> Hey [Name] -- your [specific post/thread] on [topic] is part of why I started publishing the Tokenized Credit Monitor. Issue #1 covers [one-line hook]: [link]. If any of it's wrong, I want to know that most of all.

## Notes
- **Sensitivity rule:** for MEV Capital / Re7 (xUSD history) — never reference their losses. Their pool's section, straight, same as anyone else's.
- **Farcaster variant:** same skeletons, drop the "Hey [Name] --" opener, even shorter.
- **Log rule:** every reply verbatim into episodic/ same day.
- **Volume:** 15 in week 2 (Monitor #1 recipients), then ~15–20/week. Hand-picked > volume.
