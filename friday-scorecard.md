# friday-scorecard.md — Friday session prompt (30 min cap)

Paste with: memory/working/gates.md, the week's numbers (subs, downloads, calls booked/held, replies, revenue), and this week's 10 best outputs for grading.

---

Friday scorecard. Two jobs, in order:

**Job 1 — Gates.** Compare actuals to gates.md line by line. Then one paragraph verdict, exactly one of: ON TRACK / ADJUST X (name the X) / KILL-SIGNAL WATCH (name the signal and the pre-committed response from the validation plan). Rules: if a number misses, diagnose in writing before recommending anything — messaging vs. channel vs. offer vs. market, with the evidence for the diagnosis. Conversion benchmarks: targeted traffic→email ≥5% real, <2% messaging problem (rewrite once before concluding demand problem); email→call ≥25%; call→purchase ≥20%. Do not soften misses. Do not manufacture urgency on Credentify.

**Job 2 — Evals.** Grade the 10 attached outputs against rubrics/ (Monitor section, outreach message, audit finding). Be harsh — quality drift is silent. Per output: score /10, the single biggest weakness, one line. Then: one pattern across the week's outputs worth writing to memory/lessons/.

End with the compound block: one lesson, ≤5 lines of markdown, ready to commit.
